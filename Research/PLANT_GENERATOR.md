# Parametric indoor plants

Open `Assets/PlantLab.unity` to see three generated plants in the combat encounter. The scene retains the current rider, fly, and enemy setup. Select a plant root and change its `ProceduralPlant` fields in the Inspector, then click **Regenerate and save plant meshes**. The three presets are also saved as prefabs under `Assets/Generated/Plants`. **Fruit Fly → Plants → Build Indoor Plant Lab** recreates the scene from the combat encounter and places the pots away from existing colliders, including the arena pillar; **Validate Plant Lab** checks the saved geometry, placement, and fly contact footprint.

This is an invented plant grammar inspired by the branching modules and phyllotaxis in Prusinkiewicz and Lindenmayer's [*The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants*](https://algorithmicbotany.org/papers/abop/abop.pdf) and Hanan's [parametric L-system work](https://www.algorithmicbotany.org/papers/hanan.dis1992.html). It is not a biological species reconstruction or a full string-rewriting L-system. Each node grows one, two, or three leaves in spiral, opposite, or whorled order. The spiral default advances by 137.5 degrees; seed and variation produce repeatable differences. Internode count and length, branch chance and angle, stem taper, bend, and facet twist are editable. Four to seven flat faces remain visible around each stem.

Leaf width follows a normalized two-exponent profile. `widestPoint` shifts the maximum width along the length, while `edgeFullness` changes how rapidly the outline narrows toward its ends. Leaf length, width, rise, droop, cup, and thickness are separate controls. Each closed blade has an upper and lower rendered face and collider, with veins on both sides. Mature preset blades are 3.2–3.9 world units wide and 3.8–4.8 long, larger than the playable fly's body. `tipLeafScale` reduces leaves over the final growing nodes. The gameplay fly's geometric perch rule accepts sampled upper and lower mature leaf surfaces and at least one side of each preset's faceted stem; this is not six-claw biological contact.

Each main stem also carries four visible reproductive stages at once: a top bud, an open petaled flower, a smaller developing fruit, and a ripe fruit on lower, older nodes. Petal number and radius, stalk length, and fruit radius are editable. These are stylized mathematical organs, not a simulation of pollination or a known species.

The indoor pot is a separate child. Its tapered cylindrical shell has rotated flat facets, a rim, and soil. Disable `includePot` and regenerate to place the same growth geometry at ground height for a future outdoor scene. The pot is presentation and support geometry, not part of the growth grammar.

The editor validation report is `Research/plant-lab-evaluation.json`. It checks that the combat components remain in the scene, all three presets survive as saved mesh assets, their leaf surfaces can be hit from above and below, their stems can be hit from the side, the fly's footprint rule accepts sampled contacts on all three surface types, reproductive stages and smaller tip leaves exist, pots avoid existing obstacles, and the pot-free mode starts at ground height. It does not certify a complete manual landing flight path or biologically faithful crawling animation.
